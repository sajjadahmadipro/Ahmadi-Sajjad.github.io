About Me.

I don't obsess over tools; I obsess over results.

I am a Computer Engineering student at the University of Tehran, but my education started long before university. It started with messed-up laptops, cryptic error messages, and a stubborn refusal to give up until the game launched.

That mindset—"get the job done, no matter what"—defines my engineering style today. Whether I'm diving into low-level C++ to optimize performance or architecting a sleek Web Interface, I bridge the gap between complex problems and working solutions.
